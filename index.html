✦ WHAT YOU LEARNED — THE SCIENCE BEHIND YOUR DECISIONS
1. The Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation Mass is exponential. Adding heavy components requires exponentially more propellant. Successful mission architects optimize every gram of dry mass.
2. Hohmann Transfer Orbits Reaching destinations requires precise delta-v burns at periapsis and apoapsis. Running out of delta-v leaves spacecraft stranded in useless orbits.
3. Inverse-Square Law for Solar Power Solar irradiance falls off with the square of distance ($1/r^2$). Outer planet missions require nuclear RTGs instead of solar panels.
4. Telemetry Link Margin & Antenna Gain Deep space communication relies on parabolic dish gain and ground DSN stations to maintain high-frequency command links across astronomical distances.
